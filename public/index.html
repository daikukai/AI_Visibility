How We Calculate Your Readiness Score

Our score is 100% rule-based, transparent, and reproducible. We do not use hallucinated LLM scores to judge your website.

25 pts

Crawlability

Can AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot) reach and parse your content without getting blocked by robots.txt, noindex, or JS execution barriers?

25 pts

Company Clarity

Are fundamental brand anchors present? Descriptive H1, concise meta description, clear title tags, contact phone/email, and physical/service locations.

25 pts

Q&A Content

LLMs excel at extracting direct answers. Does your domain contain structured FAQ sections, question headings ("?"), and dedicated service/pricing pages?

25 pts

Structured Data

Do you publish valid JSON-LD schemas? Organization entities with logo and sameAs social profile verification, Service/Product schemas, and Open Graph metadata.
