About the Telza Readiness Scanner

Last updated: October 2026 • User-Agent: TelzaReadinessCheck/1.0 (+https://check.telzasales.com/about-scanner)

What is this bot?

The Telza Readiness Scanner is an on-demand, lightweight website inspection tool triggered when a user enters a domain into our free public tool at check.telzasales.com.

Its sole purpose is to evaluate whether a website satisfies standard technical and architectural signals required by modern conversational AI search engines (such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini).

Crawler Behavior & Polite Constraints

  • Zero bulk crawling: We do NOT recursively crawl your website. A scan fetches at most 3 to 4 public pages (homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and optionally a single linked FAQ/About page).
  • Bandwidth & timeout caps: Each request has a hard 1 MB response cap and a strict 5–6 second timeout.
  • SSRF-safe: The scanner never accesses private, loopback, link-local, internal, or cloud-metadata IP ranges.
  • No headless browser execution: The scanner inspects only static HTTP responses and does not execute JavaScript bundles or trigger heavy browser resources.
  • No secret scraping: We only read publicly available markup and never attempt login, forms, or authenticated routes.

How to Block or Inquire

If you wish to disallow our scanner from making on-demand checks of your domain, you can include the following in your robots.txt: 

User-agent: TelzaReadinessCheck
Disallow: /
