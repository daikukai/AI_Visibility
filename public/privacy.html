Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 2026 • Telza Sales

1. Scope & Purpose

This privacy notice explains how Telza Sales collects, processes, and protects information submitted to the AI Search Readiness Check tool at check.telzasales.com.

2. What Information We Collect

  • Website URLs: Public domain addresses entered into the scanner.
  • Business Email Address: Provided voluntarily when requesting a manual AI visibility report.
  • Optional Context: Any one-line description you submit describing what your company sells.
  • Readiness Metrics: Calculated technical scores and check results for the requested domain.

3. Why We Process Your Data

We process this information solely to:

  • Generate and deliver your requested manual AI visibility report within 24–48 hours.
  • Send 1 to 2 direct, human follow-up emails regarding the report findings and Telza's AI visibility advisory services.
  • Prevent automated abuse and maintain service integrity.

We never sell your contact details or add your business email to automated bulk cold-outreach lists.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Lead submissions are securely stored using Netlify serverless infrastructure and encrypted email notification gateways. Lead records are retained for up to 12 months, after which they are safely purged unless an active client or commercial conversation is ongoing.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and international data protection laws, you have the right to request access to or permanent deletion of any personal or business contact records we hold for you.

To request removal, send an email to privacy@telzasales.com or hello@telzasales.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will locate your lead record, purge all stored copies, and confirm completion within 30 days.
