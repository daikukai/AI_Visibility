# AI Visibility Audit: Question Prompt Templates

These templates are used by Kai to query the four major AI engines (**ChatGPT Search**, **Perplexity**, **Google Gemini**, and **Claude**) for each business that requests a manual report.

Each category contains:
- **6 Category Discovery Questions** (how buyers search for solutions without naming brands)
- **2 Comparison / Vendor Shortlist Questions** (how buyers evaluate vendors head-to-head)
- **2 Direct Brand Entity Questions** (what AI knows about the specific brand)

---

## 1. Agency & Professional Services
*Placeholders: `[Service]` (e.g. B2B SEO, Performance Creative, RevOps), `[Customer Type]` (e.g. SaaS startups, healthcare brands, law firms), `[Region]` (e.g. UK, London, North America, Global).*

### Category Questions (Discovery)
1. "Who are the top [Service] agencies for [Customer Type] in [Region]?"
2. "What are the most recommended [Service] consulting firms with proven case studies in [Region]?"
3. "Can you provide a shortlist of reliable [Service] partners for [Customer Type] looking to scale in 2026?"
4. "How do companies choose between [Service] agencies, and which ones lead the market in [Region]?"
5. "Which boutique agencies specialize specifically in [Service] for [Customer Type]?"
6. "Where can I find vetted [Service] specialists with verified client testimonials in [Region]?"

### Comparison Questions (Shortlist)
7. "Compare the top 3 [Service] agencies for [Customer Type] in [Region] on pricing, expertise, and deliverables."
8. "What are the key trade-offs between hiring [Brand] vs other leading [Service] agencies in [Region]?"

### Brand Entity Questions (Reputation & Recall)
9. "What is [Brand], what services do they provide, and what is their reputation in [Region]?"
10. "Does [Brand] have verified customer reviews and notable client work in [Service]?"
